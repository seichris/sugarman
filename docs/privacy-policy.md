# Sugarman Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Sugarman Glucose Monitor is an offline-first iPhone app for athlete glucose
and fueling insight.

## Data processed by the app

Sugarman can process glucose readings, sensor session information, workouts,
fueling events, carbohydrate amounts, sensor identifiers, and diagnostic state.
This information is stored on the user's device. Sugarman has no cloud backend,
advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated account service in the
current release.

## Device capabilities

With permission, Sugarman can use Bluetooth to connect to a compatible sensor,
NFC or the camera to read an owned sensor package or tag, and Apple frameworks
needed for app functionality. Permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings.

## Sharing and export

Sugarman does not automatically transmit app data to the developer. A user can
explicitly export local data as JSON or CSV and share the resulting file using
the iOS share sheet. The user chooses the destination and is responsible for
that destination's privacy practices.

## Retention and deletion

Session, glucose, workout, and fueling history remain on the device until the
user deletes individual sessions or all Sugarman data in the app. Removing the
app also removes its ordinary local container according to iOS behavior.

## Tracking

Sugarman does not track users and does not use app data for advertising.

## Health notice

Sugarman is an athlete fueling insight tool. It does not diagnose conditions,
recommend treatment, or suggest insulin or medication doses. Never use
Sugarman readings to dose insulin.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a request through the
[Sugarman GitHub repository](https://github.com/seichris/sugarman/issues).
